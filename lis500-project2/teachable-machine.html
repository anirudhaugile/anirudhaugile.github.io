Water Bottle or Notebook?

By Anirudha Ugile and Lathika Suresh

This is our first iteration of an image classifier trained with Google Teachable Machine. It attempts to identify whether a webcam image shows a water bottle or a notebook.

Training samples

  • Original prototype: 138 water bottle images and 164 notebook images
  • Updated model: retrained with an additional Other / Neither class

The sample counts above describe the earlier two-class version. We will document the updated model's sample counts, individual contributions, and testing in the final iteration.

Try the classifier

Click Start Camera and allow webcam access. Hold a bottle or notebook up to the camera. The percentages are model predictions, not guaranteed accuracy.

If the camera cannot start, check browser permission settings and use a device with a webcam.

Testing and limitations

We are testing how the model responds to other water bottles and notebooks, new lighting conditions, and objects outside its two training categories. Since the model has only two classes, it may still label an unrelated object as one of them.
