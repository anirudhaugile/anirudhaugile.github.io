Water Bottle or Notebook?

By Anirudha Ugile and Lathika Suresh

This is our first iteration of an image classifier trained with Google Teachable Machine. It classifies webcam images as water bottle, notebook, or other / neither.

Training samples

  • Water bottle: 1,094 images
  • Notebook: 703 images
  • Other / Neither: 1,405 images
  • Total: 3,202 images

The current version has three classes. We will document how we collected the samples, each group member's contribution, and the results of testing in the final iteration.

Try the classifier

Click Start Camera and allow webcam access. Hold up a bottle, notebook, or another object to see the three predictions. The percentages are model predictions, not guaranteed accuracy.

If the camera cannot start, check browser permission settings and use a device with a webcam.

Testing and limitations

Our first model had only two classes and sometimes identified unrelated objects as bottles or notebooks. We added an Other / Neither class to address this problem. We still need to test the updated model with different objects, backgrounds, and lighting conditions. It can still make incorrect predictions.
