03 / LIMITATIONS

When the model gets it wrong

What happened in the first version?

Our original model had only two classes. It sometimes labeled a phone as a notebook and an empty chair as a water bottle, even with high confidence.

What changed?

We added an Other / Neither class and expanded the training data. That gives the model an additional possible output, but it does not eliminate mistakes or guarantee performance on unfamiliar images.

Questions to investigate: Does the model learn the objects or the room and hands around them? Does it behave differently on another device or under different lighting? These tests matter more than a high prediction score on a familiar example.
