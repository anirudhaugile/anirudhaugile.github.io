What is Implicit bias?

Implicit Bias – A thought that you might not even realize you are doing because of automatic associations you are making in your brain. Project Implicit – The Implicit Association Test, or IAT, is a means for measuring attitudes and beliefs that people may be unwilling or unable to report.

What is intersectionality?

The Concept of Intersectionality Is an Approach to Analyzing how various aspects of a person's social and political identities might combine to create unique modes of discrimination and privilege. Race, gender, class, ability, sexuality, nationality, and other identities do not exist separately from each other in people's lives.

Our thoughts after the IAT

We found one of the main strengths of the test to be how fast it was. It required participants to make rapid associations, rather than merely giving their opinion. It was interesting as a thought-provoking prompt to considering the nature of automatic responses, but we would not take a single test result to be a definitive measure of anyone's beliefs or character. An interesting question is what we do afterwards.

Intersectionality sharpens that question. Bias does not impact everyone equally. To take an analogy, a system can be just as fair in terms of overall average while having wildly different effects for groups whose overlapping identities were not considered by the designers.

Why this matters in technology

All of our software is written by human beings, trained or tested on data that has been curated by human beings, and deployed within human-run institutions. This means that technical systems have the capacity to amplify existing patterns present in society. Providing high levels of accuracy is not adequate if errors are disproportionately impacting some groups over others. Developers should ask – who is represented in the data, who is absent, who encounters errors and who has the ability to oppose a system that harms them.
