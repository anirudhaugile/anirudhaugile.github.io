TECHNOLOGY IS NOT NEUTRAL

Technology is not separate from society.

This site considers technology in terms of issues of power, bias, identity, and responsibility. We want to examine what occurs when systems that on the surface seem neutral have been influenced by human decisions, social dynamics, and unequal distribution of power.

As well as tackling these topics of implicit bias, intersectionality and challenging unfair tech norms, our project is working to explain these concepts so that if you're new to them, it's easy to understand and relate it back to tech we all use.

OUR APPROACH

Bias is not just an individual problem.

Because technology is created within social systems, issues around data, design, testing and representation all come into play. In this site we try to relate those ideas to actual technical systems and people's everyday digital lives by bringing in examples and outside resources.
