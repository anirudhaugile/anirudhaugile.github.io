Technology is not separate from society

This site looks at technology through questions of power, bias, identity, and responsibility. We are interested in what happens when systems that appear neutral are shaped by human choices, social patterns, and unequal access to power.

Our project focuses on implicit bias, intersectionality, and people who challenge unfair norms in technology. The goal is to make these ideas understandable to someone encountering them for the first time, while also connecting them to technologies we use every day.

Our approach

We do not want to treat bias as only an individual problem. Technology is designed inside larger social systems, so questions about data, design, testing, and who gets represented all matter. Throughout the site, we use examples and outside resources to connect those ideas to real technical systems and everyday digital experiences.
